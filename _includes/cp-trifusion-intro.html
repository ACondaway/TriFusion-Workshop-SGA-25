TriFusion Workshop@SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 explores the fascinating intersection of Humans 😐, Avatars 🫥, and Humanoid Robotics 🤖 in the quest for embodied intelligence. This workshop catalyzes interdisciplinary dialogue and innovation at the convergence of computer graphics, artificial intelligence, robotics, and cognitive science.

As digital avatars and humanoid robots increasingly serve as proxies and collaborators in both virtual and physical environments, there is a growing need to understand and develop embodied intelligence that can fluidly span these domains. We investigate the shared principles and enabling technologies that support the seamless transfer of perception, cognition, and motor skills across human users, digital surrogates, and robotic agents.

  • Cross-Domain Transfer

Join us in exploring the future of embodied intelligence where the boundaries between human cognition, virtual avatars, and physical robots dissolve into unified intelligence systems.
