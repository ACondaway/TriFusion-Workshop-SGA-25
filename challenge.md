## Challenge Overview

The TriFusion Challenge is a series of technical competitions designed to advance the state-of-the-art in embodied intelligence across humans, avatars, and humanoid robotics. We invite researchers and practitioners to participate in cutting-edge challenges that explore the boundaries of cross-domain intelligence transfer.

## Participation Guidelines

### Eligibility
- Open to academic institutions, research labs, and industry teams
- Teams can have 2-8 members
- Multiple submissions per team allowed across different tracks

### Submission Requirements
- **Technical Report:** 4-8 pages describing methodology and results
- **Code:** Complete implementation with documentation
- **Demo Video:** 3-5 minutes showcasing your solution
- **Reproducibility Package:** Data, models, and evaluation scripts

### Evaluation Process
1. **Automated Evaluation:** Quantitative metrics on benchmark datasets
2. **Human Evaluation:** Qualitative assessment by expert judges
3. **Live Demonstration:** Real-time system performance at the workshop
4. **Peer Review:** Technical merit assessment by the community
